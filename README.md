# Coffee Shop | 2023 Half-Time Review

## **1. Business Performance Overview**

The dashboard analyzes coffee shop sales performance during the first half of 2023 using transaction, product, and hourly sales data.

**Key KPIs**
- Total Sales: $698.81K
- Total Transactions: 149K
- Average Order Size: $4.69
- Total Stores: 3
- Total Menu Items: 80
- Best-Selling Product: Davy Jones’ Sustainable Blend

**Business Highlights**
- Coffee and Tea are the highest revenue-generating categories.
- Morning hours (8-10 AM) drive the highest customer activity and sales.
- Signature coffee beverages dominate top-selling products.
- Sales performance is concentrated around beverage products rather than packaged or branded items.

## **2. Product Performance Breakdown**

**Total Sales by Product Category** <br>
Coffee generated the highest toatl sales at $269.95K, followed by Tea at $196.41K and Bakery products at $82.32K. Beverage categories contribute the majority of overall revenue performance.

**Top 10 Best-Selling Products by Total Sales** <br>
The highest-performing products are mainly premium coffee beverages and espresso-based drinks, including:
- Davy Jones' Sustainable Blend
- Jolly Roaster's Dark Roast
- Shipwreck Latte
- Karken Cappuccino
These products consistently contribute the strongest revenue performance across the menu.

**Top Selling Product Types** <br>
Barista Espresso, brewed coffee, and latte-based beverages show the highest customer demand, while packaged products and branded items generate comparatively lower sales contribution.

**Product Insights**
- Coffee beverages are the primary revenue drivers.
- Bakery items support additional purchase behavior and upselling opportunities.
- Lower-performing categories may require promotional strategies or menu optimization.

## **3. Hourly Sales Performance Analysis**

**Peak Revenue Hours**
Sales activity is highest during the morning period between 8 AM and 10 AM, making it the most important sales window for the business.

**Customer Activity Trends**
- Sales gradually decline after midday.
- Average order size remains relatively stable throughout the day.
- Morining customer traffic is the key constributor to daily revenue generation.

## **4. Key Takeaways & Actionable Insights**

**Revenue Drivers**
- Coffee and Tea categories contribute the majority of total sales revenue.
- Signature and espresso-based beverages are the strongest-performing products.

**Customer Purchasing Behavior**
- Morning hours generate the highest sales activity.
- Cusotmers demonstrate stable purchasing patterns with consistent order values.

**Product Opportunities**
- Expanding premium beverage offerings may increase revenue growth.
- Underperforming product categories could benefit from targeted promotions or menu adjustments.

**Operational Insights**
- Staffing and inventory planning should prioritize morning peak hours.
- High-demand products should remain consistently avaliable to prevent missed sales opportunities.

## **5. Conclusion & Recommendations**
The coffee shop demonstrates strong sales performance during the first half of 2023, driven primarily by coffee beverages, tea products, and moring customer demand.

**Recommendations**
- Focus on expanding top-performing beverage products.
- Optimize staffing during high-traffic morning hours.
- Introduce beverage and bakery bundle promotions to increase average order values.
- Improve visibility and promotion of lower-performing product categories.
- Continue monitoring sales patterns to support operational and menu decisions.
